About ParkAhead

Predictive Mall Parking & Arrival Intelligence

ParkAhead is an academic machine-learning project that estimates parking occupancy at a user's expected arrival time and compares parking options with the goal of reducing the risk of arriving late to a movie, event, or other appointment.

How it works

  1. Load historical parking observations.
  2. Clean timestamps and invalid or missing records.
  3. Create time-based and historical features.
  4. Train a model and evaluate its predictions using available metrics.
  5. Estimate occupancy at the entered arrival time and rank facilities using the recommendation logic.

Technology

Python, Flask, Flask-SQLAlchemy, SQLite, pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, and joblib, with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript for the interface.

Limitations

  • Predictions use historical data rather than live parking sensors.
  • Data collected in one location may not represent parking patterns elsewhere.
  • Travel and walking times may not reflect live traffic or venue conditions.
  • A predicted open space is not guaranteed and cannot be reserved through this prototype.
